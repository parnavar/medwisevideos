Clinical & Histology Guide

Circumvallate papillae (8–12) form the V-shaped sulcus terminalis. Each papilla is surrounded by a deep trench lined by stratified squamous epithelium containing ~250 taste buds per papilla.

🔬 Von Ebner's glands secrete lingual lipase and serous fluid, flushing the trench to renew tastant stimuli.

⚡ Glossopharyngeal nerve (CN IX) innervates vallate papillae, mediating bitter, sour, and umami signals.

🧫 H&E stain: Eosinophilic (pink) = epithelium/cytoplasm | Basophilic (purple) = nuclei/taste buds
